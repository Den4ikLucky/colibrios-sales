1. Scope

The policy explains the processing of data on the ColibriOS website, its Telegram Mini App and when purchasing products through Lava.top. The project name does not indicate a separate legal entity. Mandatory rules on personal data apply, including Federal Law of the Russian Federation No. 152-FZ, when applicable.

2. What data is processed

  • Calculator and diagnostics: selected role, numerical estimates, hours, cost per hour, release percentage and process responses. The calculation is performed in the browser. No contact is required to undergo diagnostics.
  • Browser memory: anonymized draft diagnostics, selected tab and trial window time. This data is stored in localStorage of this device. Old interface marks may remain until the storage is cleared.
  • B2B application: name, specified contact, description of business, task, attempts and expected result. In local mode, the data is used only to download the file on the device and is not sent to the operator.
  • When design is enabled: email, selected product, order number, amount, currency and confirmed payment status; when issued via Telegram: confirmed account ID and information necessary for delivery of access.
  • Technical data: The hosting and payment service may process the IP address, access time, browser information and technical logs according to their own rules. Do not send passport data, passwords, bank codes or personal information of your clients.

3. Goals and reasons

Goals: perform the requested calculation; respond to a voluntary request; conclude and execute an agreement; match the payment with the order; grant and restore access; fulfill legal obligations and consider requests. Grounds: actions initiated by the user to enter into and perform a contract, applicable legal obligations, as well as separate consent when required. Consent to advertising is not included in consent to process an application.

4. Transfer and external services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Static fonts are supplied with the site; advertising pixels and analytical counters are not connected in this assembly. Hosting infrastructure technical logs are not equal to local diagnostic responses.

Lava.top and its payment partners process checkout and payment data under their own terms and policies. Full card details are entered on a secure payment page and are not transferred to the ColibriOS form. The seller receives only the information necessary for ordering, accounting, support and access.

The official Telegram Web App SDK is loaded into the Mini App. Telegram processes its platform data according to its own rules. The diagnostic result is transmitted to the bot only by a separate user action in a supported launch scenario; clicking is not considered confirmation of receipt by the bot. When paying, the server must verify the authenticity of the Telegram data.

Before enabling server data collection, the Seller determines the actual place of processing, the range of recipients, the need for localization and the conditions for cross-border transfer. This policy does not, in itself, replace notice, consent or other required actions. The sale and transfer of data for other people's advertising is not provided.

5. Storage and protection

A local draft remains on the device until the user deletes it using the application or browser settings. When downloaded, the file is saved to the device and managed by the user. The request text and contact are not written to localStorage by this build.

When server processing is connected, data is stored to the extent and duration necessary for the contract, support, mandatory accounting and protection of legal claims. At the end of the basis, the data is deleted or anonymized, unless further storage is required by law. Access control, secure transmission and control of payment notifications are used. Secret keys are not included in the site's public files.

6. Rights and appeals

The user has the right to request information about processing, clarification, blocking or deletion of data, withdraw consent and exercise other legal rights. The request is sent to the Seller’s working contact specified in the order and on the website before payment opens. Indicate your contact for a response and your order number, if available; do not send unnecessary documents. Legal response deadlines and exclusions from deletion remain the same.

Withdrawal of consent does not cancel processing that remains necessary on another legal basis. The user has the right to appeal to the authorized data protection authority or court. In diagnostics, the “Delete test responses from device” button deletes its local draft; Complete site data clearing is performed in the browser settings.

7. Changes and contact

The revision date is indicated at the beginning of the page. Significant changes to the purposes, recipients or methods of processing are disclosed prior to the relevant data collection and are accompanied by new consent where necessary. Support contact and full operator details are required prior to commercial processing.
